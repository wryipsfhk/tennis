How it works

Three steps after the final point.

The route is simple. Open a step only when you want the detail.

  1. 01

    Log the match

    Record the result and how it felt.

    Add the score, match type, satisfaction, reflection, and optional footage you want to keep together.

  2. 02

    Review your movement

    Look back at visible movement patterns.

    Review rotation, balance, stance, and swing windows from your video, with findings attached to the matching moment.

  3. 03

    Choose what comes next

    Keep one focused training direction.

    Confirm the goal and practical drills that belong in your plan instead of letting the review disappear.

Why does AcePoint begin with an empty journal?

There are no invented sample records. Every result, trend, review, goal, and drill comes from information you add yourself—and you decide which suggestions become part of your training plan.
